Buyer and staff workflow verification

GOAL
Implement and verify the buyer/staff confidential access workflows before further production readiness work.

FILES ADDED

- scripts/sprint9-workflow-gate.mjs

WORKFLOW COVERAGE
1. Unauthenticated buyer document list and direct download must return 401.
2. Expired grants must be denied for both list and download.
3. Revoked grants must be denied.
4. Active grants may access only clean documents for currently published, approved opportunities, subject to access tier.
5. Downloads must be private/no-store and logged.
6. Pending/rejected scan-state documents must not be listed or downloaded.
7. Staff NDA endpoints must reject requests without a valid Cloudflare Access identity.
